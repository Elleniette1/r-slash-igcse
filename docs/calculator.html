How PUM is Calculated

GradePUM rangeNotes

A* grade (A-Level only, PUM ≥ 90)

PUM = 90 + (Raw − A* threshold) × 10 / (Max mark − A* threshold)

All other grades (PUM < 90, or < 80 for AS)

The grade above threshold is the next grade's boundary (e.g. for grade B it is the A threshold; for grade A on A-Level it is the A* threshold).

PUM = Grade lower PUM + (Raw − Grade threshold) × 10 / (Grade above threshold − Grade threshold)
